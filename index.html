A little about me

Hi, I'm Vishal. I study Computer Science and Data Science at Rutgers, and I'm happiest working on the parts of software most people never see: the queues, schedulers, and pipelines that quietly keep everything else running.

I've spent the last two summers at Capital One, first in McLean and then in New York, building backend systems for data and AI. It's where I figured out that I love building the things other engineers end up depending on.

I also really like teaching. I TA Data Structures at Rutgers and led a section for Stanford's Code in Place, and watching an idea finally click for someone never gets old.

Away from a keyboard, I'm usually at the gym, out on a run, playing volleyball, or losing one too many blitz games (I'm working on it). I'm also an Eagle Scout, which probably explains why this site looks like a trail map.
